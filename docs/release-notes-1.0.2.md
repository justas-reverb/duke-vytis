**Duke Vytis and the Quest for New Lands** -- a pixel-art tower climber. Run, jump and bounce off the walls up an endless tower through twelve worlds before the rising flames catch you.

### New in 1.0.2: phones, again

- **The game fills a phone's screen.** No more wide walls down both sides: on a wide phone the tower itself spans the screen, with the menus and the score at their usual size.
- **A joystick** at the bottom left runs the Duke, and moves through menus and options. **MOVE WITH** in the options swaps it for the < and > buttons.
- **No black screen at the start** on Android: a loading screen with the game's shield, and the music waits until the game is on the screen.
- **Smoother on phones**: a phone's frame now takes well under half the drawing it did, and the speed streaks are toned down.
- **In a phone's browser, iPhone included** (https://justas-reverb.github.io/duke-vytis/): held upright it asks you to turn the phone sideways.

### Windows

- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.2-setup.exe`** -- the installer. Adds a Duke Vytis shortcut to your desktop and Start menu; no administrator rights needed. It updates an earlier version in place.
- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.2-portable.exe`** -- or play without installing: one file, double-click it.

Windows will probably say *"Windows protected your PC"*: the game is not code-signed, so Windows does not know it yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A keyboard, or any standard gamepad.

### Android (test build)

- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.2.apk`** -- open it on your phone and allow the install when Android asks (it is not from the Play Store). It installs over an earlier version and keeps your records. Hold the phone sideways. Android 7 or newer.

### iPhone and iPad

Open **https://justas-reverb.github.io/duke-vytis/** in Safari and turn the phone sideways. For full screen, tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*, and play from the icon.

### How to play

`←` `→` to run, `Space` to jump -- hold it to chain jumps, and tap it in mid-air with the SPEED bar full for a double jump. On a phone, the joystick and the SPACE button. Bounce off the walls, keep moving, and climb before the fire catches you. The full guide is in the [README](https://github.com/justas-reverb/duke-vytis#how-to-play).
