**Duke Vytis and the Quest for New Lands** -- a pixel-art tower climber. Run, jump and bounce off the walls up an endless tower through twelve worlds before the rising flames catch you.

### Windows

- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.0-setup.exe`** -- the installer. Adds a Duke Vytis shortcut to your desktop and Start menu; no administrator rights needed.
- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.0-portable.exe`** -- or play without installing: one file, double-click it.

Windows will probably say *"Windows protected your PC"*: the game is not code-signed, so Windows does not know it yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A keyboard, or any standard gamepad.

### Android (test build)

- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.0.apk`** -- open it on your phone and allow the install when Android asks (it is not from the Play Store). Hold the phone sideways; the game draws its own touch keys. Android 7 or newer. A test build: played on an emulator, not yet on many real phones.

### How to play

`←` `→` to run, `Space` to jump -- hold it to chain jumps, and tap it in mid-air with the SPEED bar full for a double jump. Bounce off the walls, keep moving, and climb before the fire catches you. The full guide is in the [README](https://github.com/justas-reverb/duke-vytis#how-to-play).
