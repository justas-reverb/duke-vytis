**Duke Vytis and the Quest for New Lands** -- a pixel-art tower climber. Run, jump and bounce off the walls up an endless tower through twelve worlds before the rising flames catch you.

### New in 1.0.1: made for phones

- **Play in your browser, iPhone included**: **https://justas-reverb.github.io/duke-vytis/** -- open it in Safari, turn the phone sideways, and for full screen tap Share, then *Add to Home Screen*, and play from the icon.
- **The whole screen.** On a wide phone the game now fills the screen edge to edge, the camera's cut-out included -- no black bars.
- **A title screen for thumbs**: a big TAP TO CLIMB, and OPTIONS, STATS, REPLAYS and HELP buttons.
- **Only the keys a screen needs**, and **TOUCH KEYS** in the options to make the run keys bigger or smaller (80% to start).
- **Smoother**: a phone plays at a steady 60 frames a second (FRAME CAP in the options can take it higher), paced evenly on 120 Hz screens.

### Windows

- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.1-setup.exe`** -- the installer. Adds a Duke Vytis shortcut to your desktop and Start menu; no administrator rights needed. It updates 1.0.0 in place.
- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.1-portable.exe`** -- or play without installing: one file, double-click it.

Windows will probably say *"Windows protected your PC"*: the game is not code-signed, so Windows does not know it yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A keyboard, or any standard gamepad.

### Android (test build)

- **`Duke-Vytis-1.0.1.apk`** -- open it on your phone and allow the install when Android asks (it is not from the Play Store). It installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your records. Hold the phone sideways. Android 7 or newer. A test build: played on an emulator and one real phone so far.

### How to play

`←` `→` to run, `Space` to jump -- hold it to chain jumps, and tap it in mid-air with the SPEED bar full for a double jump. On a phone, the same keys are on the screen. Bounce off the walls, keep moving, and climb before the fire catches you. The full guide is in the [README](https://github.com/justas-reverb/duke-vytis#how-to-play).
